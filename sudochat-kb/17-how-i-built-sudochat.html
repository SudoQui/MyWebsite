# 17. How did I build SudoChat?

**Author:** Mustafa Siddiqui  
**Source type:** First-person authored response  
**Canonical recruiter question:** How was SudoChat built?

> This source is intentionally written in my first-person perspective. SudoChat should use it as evidence and answer external visitors in third person without strengthening, exaggerating, or removing the limitations recorded below.

## Direct answer
I built SudoChat as a practical Copilot Studio proof of concept to demonstrate how a controlled, evidence based AI assistant could answer questions about me without simply relying on the general knowledge of an LLM.

The system was designed around a curated knowledge base, retrieval and grounding, explicit limitations, and a user interface that makes the assistant's purpose clear. The objective was not to build a general chatbot. It was to build a bounded assistant that can answer a defined set of questions, show why those answers are supported, and avoid inventing claims when the evidence is insufficient.
